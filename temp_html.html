LOCAL CAT ANNOUNCES PRESIDENTIAL CAMPAIGN; POLLING IMMEDIATELY OUTPERFORMS ALL HUMAN CANDIDATES

Platform of 'more naps and fewer Mondays' resonates across party lines
BY POLITICAL CORRESPONDENT V. SMYTHE
Puzzled pundits publicly picked a proud, pompous president who promised premier pancakes, but promptly produced paltry, painful politics.
Weaver looking thoughtfully into the distance, presumably practicing pronunciation.

A three-year-old domestic short-hair named Chairman Whiskers officially announced a presidential candidacy on Tuesday morning in a press statement delivered, according to aides, 'largely through a sustained period of eye contact and then walking away.'

Despite an unconventional campaign launch, early polling data shows the feline candidate commanding a remarkable 43% of the decided vote — a figure that leaves every declared human candidate trailing by double digits.

The campaign platform, reconstructed by political analysts from observable behavioral data, appears to center on five key pillars: extended rest periods for all citizens, the abolition of Mondays, mandatory treats distributed by government officials at 3pm daily, an aggressive posture toward geopolitical rivals described only as 'the red dot,' and unspecified structural reforms to the nation's furniture.

'The appeal is actually quite straightforward,' noted one political scientist who declined to be named. 'The electorate is tired. The cat represents something very specific: the opportunity to be completely unbothered. People find that enormously attractive right now.'

ALSO OF NOTE

Dogs Announce Counter-Campaign

A coalition of neighborhood dogs announced a rival campaign within the hour, pledging 'maximum enthusiasm for literally everything.' Early polling suggests strong name recognition but concerns about 'consistency of message'.
